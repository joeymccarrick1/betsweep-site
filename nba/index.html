How to read the board?
Score
The model's points for each team tonight. The posted line beside the tip time is the sportsbook line ESPN shows, named there; it is not FanDuel's.
Bar and number
Projected points, with the bar scaled to the game's top scorer. The tag after it is his place among tonight's players at his position.
Position
PG SG SF PF C, from ESPN's depth charts: the position where a player stands highest on his team's chart. ESPN's box scores list only guard, forward or center this season, so a player on no chart is shown as G, F or C. What an opponent allows is still counted for guards, forwards and centers.
Minutes
The model starts from minutes. Each team has 240 to hand out, so when players are out their minutes go to the ones who are left. That is the biggest thing the test results rewarded.
Stat chips
Tap a player. Each chip is a projection; its color is how much the opponent allows to his position in that stat, #1 (green) allows the most and #30 (red) the least.
Chance of each line
How often the model expects him to reach that many. There is no price beside it yet, so it cannot say whether a bet is good value.
Out tonight
From ESPN's injury report. A player listed doubtful is treated as out; questionable and probable players stay in, tagged.
