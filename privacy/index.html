BetSweep · Privacy Policy

What betsweep.co collects (almost nothing): no accounts, no trackers, slips and settings stay in your browser; the only data we hold is what you email us.

Terms of UsePrivacyResponsible gambling & disclosures

Effective October 27, 2026 · BetSweep LLC · support@betsweep.co

Short version: betsweep.co works without an account, runs no ad trackers and no analytics scripts, and keeps your slips and settings in your browser. If you choose to sign in (optional), we store your email address and the things you build on the site so they follow you between devices - nothing else.

What we collect

  • Unless you sign in or post, nothing you type on the site is sent to us. Parlay slips, Fantasy rosters, DFS lineups and your settings are stored in your browser's local storage on your device and never leave it except when you choose to open a sportsbook link or share a post.
  • Sleeper usernames. If you enter a Sleeper username on the Fantasy page, your browser asks Sleeper's public API for that account's leagues and rosters directly. BetSweep's servers are not involved and we do not see or store it.
  • Optional accounts. If you sign in, we store your email address, the time you accepted these terms and confirmed you are 21 or older, and the items you build on the site (saved betslips, the builder slip, hand-built fantasy teams, trade scenarios, the Sleeper username and league you entered). They are kept with our authentication and database provider, Supabase (servers in the United States), tied to your account and readable only by you and by us for support. Signing out leaves a copy in your browser; to delete the account and everything stored with it, email support@betsweep.co from the signed-in address and it is removed within 30 days. We do not sell or share it and we send no marketing email unless you ask for it.
  • Social posts. If you post a slip to the Social page, the handle, title, legs and price you entered, the time, and the upvotes and reports on it are stored with Supabase and shown publicly (your email is not). Upvotes and reports record which account made them so each account counts once. Deleting a post removes it; deleting your account removes your posts, votes and reports.
  • Email. If you write to support@betsweep.co we keep the message so we can answer it and fix what you reported.
  • Server logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log IP addresses and request details for security and operations under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or use those logs.

Cookies and tracking

We set no cookies and run no advertising or analytics trackers. Pages load web fonts from Google Fonts and a small image-rendering library from cdnjs (Cloudflare); those providers see the ordinary request data a browser sends when it fetches a file. When you tap a sportsbook button you leave our site; the sportsbook's own privacy policy applies from there, and if the link is an affiliate link the sportsbook or its network may record that you arrived from BetSweep (see disclosures).

The installable app

Adding BetSweep to your home screen installs a service worker that caches pages on your device for offline use. It stores nothing about you and can be removed by deleting the app or clearing site data.

Children

The site is for adults of legal betting age. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and the site is not directed at them.

Your choices

Clear your browser's site data to remove everything BetSweep stored on your device. Email us to have any message you sent deleted. Because we hold no account data, there is nothing else to export or erase.

Changes and contact

If we ever add accounts, payments, analytics or email lists, this page will say so before it happens. Questions: support@betsweep.co. BetSweep LLC, Tennessee.
