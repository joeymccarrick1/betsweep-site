How we get the number

Each player's chance of the game's first touchdown is his share of the projected touchdowns in that game: his projected rushing + receiving TD over both teams' total, times 94.6% (the remaining 5.4% covers a defensive or special-teams first TD and games with no TD). That is the whole model: team strength, pace and game script are already inside the projections, so the share is the clean way to convert them. Tested on every 2025 and 2026 game since Week 3 (317 games): it beats an even split by a wide margin and is calibrated top to bottom, and no extra tilt toward the favorite or the top option improved it.
