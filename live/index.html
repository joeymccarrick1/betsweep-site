How this page works

Picks shows your slip, anything you pinned (☆), the best parlay legs and the model's clearest edges for each game; Every leg shows the whole board. During a game each leg shows the live stat against its line and the model's chance from here. Pin the legs you bet and "My week" keeps the running record you can copy or download when the games are done.

Slips: every slip here is tracked live as one parlay. Tap + on any leg to put it in a slip, build a new one, or paste a bet you already placed and the page matches it to this week's legs.

Live score, clock, possession and box scores come from ESPN's public scoreboard, refreshed every 30 seconds while a game is on. "From here" pushes the pregame model forward with the time left: yardage and reception legs assume the rest of the game runs at the pregame rate with the pregame spread of outcomes; a touchdown leg keeps the pregame scoring rate for the minutes left; first-TD legs settle on the game's first touchdown; game lines use the same margin and total spreads as the pregame price. After halftime the rest-of-game rates tilt with the score (a team down 7+ throws more, a team up 10+ runs more) and a team in the red zone lifts its players' touchdown chances. None of it knows about an in-game injury, a blowout that benches a starter, or a team sitting on the ball - read the situation line before you trust the number. ☆ pins a leg to the top of its game (saved on this device); + puts a leg in a slip; every slip (the builder slip, slips you build here, slips you import) is tracked at the top of the page as one parlay with the model's chance from here, the ticket price or the price the legs imply, and a fair price from here; slips live on this device only; rows flash when their status changes; "on pace / behind pace" compares the current stat to the share of the game played.
