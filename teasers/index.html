A teaser moves every spread and total in the slip 6, 6.5 or 7 points your way and prices two legs at one number. The chance of each teased leg comes from the same model the boards use (final margin spread 12.6 points, totals 12.9, centred halfway between the model and FanDuel's line). Legs that cross both 3 and 7 are the ones the sharp money has teased for decades. Type your book's two-leg price and the edges update.
