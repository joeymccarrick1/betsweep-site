## Coverage deltas: negative percentages (fixed 10/22)
- flags.py read Clay's T/R delta "12%" as 12 and divided by 100 only when it was > 1, so "-2%" stayed -2 (shown as "-200%") and every
  receiver with a small negative target delta and F/R <= -0.15 was flagged Tough coverage (about 7 Week 7 receivers, e.g. -7% / -0.16).
  Now abs() > 1 is divided. Tough coverage is still a watch flag (no parlay gating) until a graded week supports it.
- TNF Week 7 check: Burden +6% / +0.21 (just under the Coverage edge bar) went 4-130-1; Odunze -2% / -0.10 went 4-69; A.J. Brown +10% / -0.11 went 3-48.

## Receiver face-off on every game card (faceoff.py, owner 10/22)
- Collapsible "Receiver face-off" under the Coverage line: per team, up to 5 WR/TE side by side - targets/game, target share over the
  last 3, catch rate, YAC/catch, aDOT, Clay corner read (▲/▼ = the Coverage flag bar, △/▽ leaning), 80+ yard games, chance of 80+ and
  100+ receiving yards, TD % (leg board). Best in the room in bold green. Display only - no projection, leg % or parlay changes.
- Usage comes from nflverse stats_player_week_<season>.csv, downloaded by faceoff.load() into in/ on every build (weeks before this one
  only). If the download fails it reuses the cached file; with neither, the block is left off (note printed) - not a build failure.
- Ceiling %: logistic curve in log(projected receiving yards) (faceoff.CURVE), fit on 2022-24 WR/TE games and checked on 2025; blended
  toward FanDuel's price (calib.shrink) only when FanDuel prices 80+ / 100+, else shown with "est". Re-fit each offseason.

## Game-script flags read the wrong columns (fixed 10/23)
- flags.py loaded 'V2 - Game Scripts' rows one column early (r[4] = margin taken as the total), so every script flag was shifted:
  "Shootout" fired on a game's CLOSE-LOW-SCORING % (CLE-TEN, projected 35 points, showed "51+ point shootout 44%"), "Lead script"
  read the TEAM-A-BY-10 % as lead and "Trail script" read the market total as a % ("GB trailing by 10+ in 53%" was the 53.5 total).
  Now (a, b, pa, pb, total, lead, close, shootout, trail) = r[0], r[1], r[2], r[3], r[5], r[6], r[7], r[8], r[9], the same columns
  build_boards.py / build_builder.py use. Week 7 inputs: 35 Shootout / 34 Trail / 31 Lead flags before -> 0 / 16 / 14 after.
- Trail script also drives the home-page rushing exclusion and the best-parlay penalty, so those picks change with the fix.

## Best parlay wording + model total on every card (owner 10/23: "close game, under" next to "take the over 41.5")
- The best parlay now says "does best if <ending> (N% likely)" instead of "built for <ending>": the slip maximises its chance over all
  four endings, so the ending it does best in is often not the most likely one (IND-MIN: best if MIN by 10+, 33%; most likely close, 35%).
- The game legend reads "How the model sees the game ending · model total X vs market Y". The "close game, under 51" bucket is about
  the 51-point shootout line, not the market total - IND-MIN projects 43.8 vs a 41.5 market, so the over is the model's side.
- Checked 10/23: QB matchup adjustments are already in the projection tabs ('Pass Yds Matchup', e.g. Daniel Jones 240.6 x 90.2% vs MIN =
  217). nflverse 2019-25: QBs vs a bottom-six pass-yards defense ran 15 yds below their norm relative to other games (RB rushing -8,
  WR -1, TE -2), a fit of x0.94 at rank 32 - the sheet's adjustment is at least that strong, so no change.
