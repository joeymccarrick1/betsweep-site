DraftKings salaries load automatically for the main Sunday slate - nothing to do. FanDuel main-slate salaries load automatically too once BetSweep posts them for the week. For FanDuel (or a DraftKings showdown / other slate), load the site's own player list: on FanDuel open a contest and use Download players list above the player pool; on DraftKings use Export to CSV on the draft screen. Drop the file on this box, choose it, or paste its contents. It stays in your browser - nothing is uploaded anywhere. Salaries change during the week, so reload before you enter.

On a phone: the FanDuel and DraftKings apps have no download button. Open fanduel.com (or draftkings.com) in Safari or Chrome, switch to the desktop site (Safari: tap aA → Request Desktop Website; Chrome: ⋮ → Desktop site), log in, open the contest and tap Download players list. The file lands in Files → Downloads (iPhone) or Downloads (Android); come back here and tap Choose file.

    How it works. Every number here is the BetSweep model's Week 9 projection (the same one behind the matchup boards and the parlay builder), converted to the site's scoring, with injury designations and the snap-share trim already applied. Salaries come from the player list you load, matched to the model by name and team; players the model does not project (deep bench, injured all year) are listed in the load message and skipped. A player is scored only if he projects - we never fill a missing player with zero.

    Single game (MVP)
    for Showdown / single-game contests: pick the game, and the lineup is one MVP (Captain on DraftKings) plus FLEX slots, any position including K and D/ST. The MVP scores 1.5x and costs 1.5x salary on both sites (read straight from the player list when it has the MVP / Captain salary). The optimizer tries every realistic MVP, fills the rest exactly under the cap, and keeps at least one player from each team. A DraftKings Showdown file switches this on by itself. ★ on a FLEX row makes that player the MVP.
