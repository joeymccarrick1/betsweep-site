How to read BetSweep

What every color, number and chip on the site means - in about three minutes.

Start here

BetSweep projects every NFL skill player from usage, matchups, game script and injuries, then prices every FanDuel prop against that projection. The price is never an input to the projection - it is only what we compare against.

  • Matchup Boards - one card per game: projected score, how the game is likely to go, every player's projected stats colored by the matchup, and a model-priced same-game slip.
  • Parlay Builder - every FanDuel prop on the slate with the model's chance; build a slip and send it to FanDuel, DraftKings or Hard Rock.
  • Players - one page per player: his lines at all three books, how often he has cleared them, matchup, snap share and game log.
  • Receipts - everything is frozen before kickoff and graded after. The misses stay up.

The color scale

Every rank on the site uses one scale, from green (good for the over) to red (bad for the over). Ranks run 1-32.

#1 - green: allows the most / produces the most#32 - red: allows the least / produces the least
  • Defense rank: #1 = the defense that allows the most of that stat to the position (a soft matchup). From the sheet's matchup tab, season and last 3 weeks.
  • Offense rank: #1 = the offense whose players at that position produce the most of that stat.
  • Roughly: 1-10 green, 11-21 gray / neutral, 22-32 red.

Reading a matchup

GB #32vsCAR #1 Green Bay's RBs rank #32 in rushing yards produced; Carolina's defense allows the #1 most to RBs.

The left side is his offense, the right side is the defense he faces, both for the stat in question. Green on both sides is the friendliest spot for an over; red on both, the toughest. A player can be in a soft matchup (green right side) on an offense that doesn't produce that stat (red left side) - the pill shows both so you can see that tension.

The Parlay Builder's player panel and each board's Matchup by stat section show every stat separately, season and last 3 weeks. The builder's green matchups filter keeps overs where both sides are top 10 (or the offense is middle of the pack and the defense is top 5). It is optional: in our backtest, hot players in green spots went over an average-based projection slightly less often, so it is a filter you choose, not a pick.

Model %, book %, edge

NumberWhat it means
Model %The chance the leg hits as the site shows it: the model's raw number blended 50/50 with FanDuel's price. Blending keeps the number honest - raw model chances at the extremes graded too confident.
Book %The chance FanDuel's price implies (including its margin). A -150 price is 60%.
EdgeModel % minus book %, in points. Positive means the model thinks the price is too long. Because of the blend, a +2.5 edge on the site is a +5 gap between the raw model and the book.
This seasonOn player pages: how many of his games this season cleared that exact line.

Nothing is called a lock. A 60% leg loses 4 times in 10.

Flags and chips

Soft matchupTrending downLove returns
  • Green chips help the over: a soft matchup, a growing role, a high game total.
  • Red chips hurt it: falling usage, a tough matchup, a line he has rarely cleared.
  • Amber chips are things to check: a teammate returning, a questionable tag, a role that grew only because someone else was hurt.

Tap a chip row for the full sentence. On the betslip, the amber ! next to a leg opens the same notes; legs with a risk flag are never used in our listed parlays.

Game cards: script and confidence

The colored bar under each scoreboard splits the game into four likely endings (for example "close, low scoring" or "home team by 10+") with the chance of each. Tap a segment to see the plays that fit that ending.

High Medium Low Confidence grades how much can go wrong with our read of the game: a big gap to the betting line, the line moving during the week, a quarterback change, a key player newly out. Low-confidence games open with the reasons showing.

Best parlay on each card is the 2-4 leg FanDuel slip, priced between -110 and +500, that the model thinks is most likely to cash.

Model's Best, top plays, promoted bets

  • Model's Best - three tiers tracked separately: even (prices near even money where the model is 8+ points off the price), TD (anytime TDs at +200 or shorter where the raw model beats the price) and safer (calibrated 65%+). Each is graded on the season page.
  • Promoted bets on the home page are slips BetSweep posts. They are graded at the posted price on the receipts.

The betslip

The + next to any leg adds it to one slip shared by every page. The slip shows the parlay's FanDuel price next to the model's chance and edge.

  • Post to BetSweep Social shares it on the Social feed; Short link copies a link anyone can open.
  • Signed in, your slips follow you to every device - and you can turn on alerts when a player on a saved slip is ruled out.

The other pages
