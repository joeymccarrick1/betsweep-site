Every FanDuel player prop on the Week 9 slate, priced against the model. Filter to what you like, tap legs into a slip, and see the model's fair odds for the whole parlay next to FanDuel's. Built Sat 11/7 12:40 PM CT.

Model %
How often BetSweep expects this leg to hit: the model's history-adjusted hit rate for that line, blended half-and-half with what FanDuel's price implies. The blend is there because on the graded pre-kickoff legs the raw model ran overconfident where it disagreed with the price; the half-way number has scored better two weeks running.
Book %
How often it has to hit for FanDuel's price to break even. −110 means 52.4%; +200 means 33.3%. The book builds its margin in here.
Edge
Model % minus Book %, in percentage points. +7.0 means the model thinks the leg hits 7 points more often than the price requires — you're being paid more than the risk deserves. Negative means FanDuel is charging more than the leg is worth. Rough guide: +1 and up is value, +2.5 and up is strong value, between −1 and +1 is a fair price, below −1 is a pass. Edge is the main sort because it's the number that makes money over time, not the hit rate on its own.
Fair
The odds the model would set for the whole slip. If FanDuel's price is longer than fair, the parlay has edge; the "take it at … or better" line leaves a 5% cushion for line movement.
EV per $1
Expected profit per dollar staked if the model is right: model hit chance × payout − 1. Positive is good; it is an average over many bets, not a promise on this one.
Last 5 dots
Did he clear the selected line in each of his last five games, newest first. Tap for the full panel and what the defense has allowed.
Game lines
The six buttons on each scorecard are FanDuel's moneyline, spread and total. The model prices them from its projected score, worked out under each game ending and mixed by the script odds, then pulled halfway toward FanDuel's no-vig price because the model's team scores have not been back-tested against closing lines yet. The small number is the edge in percentage points. "Mkt" means a consensus price from the schedule feed, not FanDuel's. A spread or total where model and market differ by 4+ points carries a risk flag.
Live / Final
At kickoff a game's legs lock (you can still remove them from your slip) and the card shows LIVE with the score and last play; once it goes final it drops below the games still to come.
