Score strip
Projected points for each team, then the four game-script odds under the score: team A wins by 10+, close and low-scoring (under 51), shootout (close, 51+), team B wins by 10+. The widest bar is the script the model thinks is most likely.
Game strip
Away @ home, kickoff (CT), venue and roof, the kickoff-hour forecast (icon = sky; temp, wind, precip chance and a LOW / MED / HIGH risk tag — wind 15+ mph or rain hurts passing and kicking legs most), then the market spread and total next to the model's; amber when they disagree by more than 3 points.
PPR
The model's projected fantasy points (point-per-reception scoring) and the player's rank at his position this week. The bar is scaled to the top player in that game.
Stat chips
Each chip is a projected stat (yards, receptions, TDs). Its color is the opponent defense's rank against that exact stat for that position this season: #1 allows the most (green) → #32 allows the least (red). The small L3 number is the same rank over the last three weeks, so you can spot a defense that is better or worse than its season number.
FD note
FanDuel's main line for that stat (the rung priced nearest −112) and how far the model sits above (+) or below (−) it. A big gap either way is worth a second look before betting.
Leg
The best FanDuel leg for that player per the model: market and line · FanDuel odds · edge. A ! means the model attached a warning to that leg (thin sample, questionable tag, projection far from his average, and so on).
Edge
Model % minus book %, in percentage points. Model % is how often the model expects the leg to hit (adjusted for the player's own history at that line); book % is how often it has to hit for FanDuel's price to break even (−110 = 52.4%, +200 = 33.3%). +7.0 means the model thinks the leg hits 7 points more often than the price requires — you are being paid more than the risk deserves. Negative means FanDuel is charging more than the leg is worth. Rough scale: +2 and up is value, +5 and up is strong value, −2 to +2 is a fair price, below −2 is a pass. Edge, not hit rate, is what makes money over time.
Script plays
Legs that get noticeably better if the most-likely game script happens (a blowout feeds the favorite's running game; a shootout feeds receivers), with the model's odds under that script.
BetSweep slip
Up to three clean legs from the game, priced together with the model's same-game correlations (receptions ↔ receiving yards, QB ↔ his receivers). "Fair" is what the model would pay; "take it at … or better" leaves a 5% cushion for line movement. Compare it to FanDuel's SGP quote, not to the legs multiplied.
Role tags
ROLE UP, AUTO QB SUCCESSION and similar mark players the model has promoted because of injuries ahead of them. Out this week lists the players removed (IR / ruled out), so you can see where targets and carries are moving.
Best parlay
The 2–4-leg FanDuel slip the model rates most likely to cash in the −110 to +500 window, built so every leg fits the same game ending ("built for"). Player legs come from the script strips; the game's moneyline, spread and total can join (at most one margin leg and one total leg). Game lines are priced under each ending from the projected score and pulled halfway toward FanDuel's no-vig price, because the model's team scores are not yet back-tested against closing lines; a 4+ point model-vs-market gap keeps a game line out. ★ marks the best leg; ⚠ a leg with a risk flag.
Live / Final
At kickoff the card's Add buttons lock and a LIVE badge and ticker appear (score, clock, possession, last play). When the game is final the card drops below the games still to come.
Game-day badges
On Sundays: CONFIRMED OUT (struck through, dropped from slips), PROPS PULLED (FanDuel took his lines down — treat as doubtful), ACTIVE.
