How saving and grading work

Save slip in the Parlay Builder, the Live page, or Tail on a promoted bet puts a slip here with its Bet on FanDuel button until kickoff. After the games it is graded from the box scores, the same way we grade our own legs. Units are at the slip's own odds and stake; a push drops the leg. Totals and first-TD legs show as pending until that grading lands.

    Bankroll

    Private to you. Saved slips grade into it automatically; add anything you bet elsewhere below.

    How the bankroll works

    It is kept in this browser and, when you sign in, in your own account - no one else can see it. Slips from Save slip, Tail, or the FanDuel screenshot import grade into it. Your unit resets every Tuesday to a set share of the bankroll, so it grows when you win and shrinks when you lose.

    Who your open slips depend on. Every player in at least two of this week's saved slips, with the stake riding on him and what you would stop being able to win if he has a bad night. Only slips saved here count - tap Tail on a promo or Save slip in the builder for the bets you place.
