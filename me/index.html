My BetSweep

Bankroll

Private to you: kept in this browser and, when you sign in, in your own account - no one else can see it. Saved slips grade into it automatically (Save slip, Tail, or the FanDuel screenshot import); add anything you bet elsewhere below. Your unit resets every Tuesday to a set share of the bankroll, so it grows when you win and shrinks when you lose.

Your record

Every slip you save - from the Parlay Builder's Save slip, the Live page, or Tail on a promoted bet - sits here with its Bet on FanDuel button until kickoff, then is graded from the box scores after the games, the same way we grade our own legs. Units at the slip's odds and stake; a push drops the leg. Totals and first-TD legs show as pending until that grading lands.

    Import a FanDuel slip

    Took the bet on FanDuel? Screenshot the betslip (or the bet under My Bets), then choose the picture here or paste it. Your phone reads it - the picture never leaves this device - and the slip is saved with the rest: graded after the games, counted in your exposure, and watched by your slip alerts.

    Tap and hold here, then tap Paste.

    Exposure this week

    Who your open slips depend on. Every player in at least two of this week's saved slips, with the stake riding on him and what you would stop being able to win if he has a bad night. Only slips saved here count - tap Tail on a promo or Save slip in the builder for the bets you place.

      Slip alerts

      Get a notification on this device when a player on one of your saved slips (or your open betslip) is ruled Out or Doubtful before kickoff - with a swap idea from the same game - plus, if you want them, live updates on your saved slips during games and a Thursday note when the Model's Best picks are up. Alerts follow the slips saved to your account, so sign in first.

      Email digest

      The morning brief by email on game days (Thursday and Sunday, 9:20 AM CT): the weather watch, the news that moved the model and the top looks. Same text as the push, for people who never allow notifications.
