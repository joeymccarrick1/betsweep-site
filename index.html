How it works

Model first

Projections come from usage, matchups, game script and injuries — the price is only used to find where FanDuel is off. Odds are never the input.

Edge, not hype

Every leg shows the model's hit rate next to what the price requires. The difference is the edge; +2 is value, +5 is strong. Nothing is called a lock.

Frozen, then graded

Projections and legs are snapshotted before kickoff and graded after. The receipts page is the scoreboard — including the misses.

Built to bet from

Fade lines, set a target payout, check a player's last five and the defense's, then send the slip to FanDuel in one tap.
