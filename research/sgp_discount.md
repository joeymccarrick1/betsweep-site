# FanDuel's same-game discount (measured)

30 same-game slips with FanDuel's own price, Weeks 7-10: 16 we posted (the promo tab) and 14 the owner priced on his
betslip without posting (screenshots of 11/14). "Share of the multiplied payout" = FanDuel's
payout on $1 divided by what the legs multiplied would pay.

| Legs in one game | Quotes | Share of the multiplied payout (low - median - high) |
|---|---|---|
| 2-3 legs | 2 | 80% - 92% - 103% |
| 4 legs | 18 | 58% - 87% - 118% |
| 5-6 legs | 4 | 59% - 68% - 82% |
| 7+ legs | 6 | 6% - 28% - 61% |

Fit: FanDuel's price for n legs in one game = exp(-0.001457 x (n-1)^3) / (FanDuel's implied chances joined with our same-game correlations).
That multiplier by leg count: 2 legs 100%, 3 legs 99%, 4 legs 96%, 5 legs 91%, 6 legs 83%, 7 legs 73%, 8 legs 61%, 9 legs 47%, 10 legs 35%.
Median miss (log price): legs multiplied 0.19; old estimate (0.85 / joint + 0.15) 0.10; new estimate, each slip left out of its own fit 0.06. By length, new estimate: 2-4 legs 0.03 (20 slips); 5+ legs 0.15 (10 slips).
