# FanDuel's same-game discount (measured)

16 same-game slips we posted with FanDuel's own price on the tab, Weeks 7-10. "Share of the multiplied payout" = FanDuel's
payout on $1 divided by what the legs multiplied would pay.

| Legs in one game | Quotes | Share of the multiplied payout (low - median - high) |
|---|---|---|
| 2-3 legs | 1 | 80% - 80% - 80% |
| 4 legs | 5 | 58% - 87% - 118% |
| 5-6 legs | 4 | 59% - 68% - 82% |
| 7+ legs | 6 | 6% - 28% - 61% |

Fit: FanDuel's price for n legs in one game = exp(-0.01043 x (n-1)^2) / (FanDuel's implied chances joined with our same-game correlations).
That multiplier by leg count: 2 legs 99%, 3 legs 96%, 4 legs 91%, 5 legs 85%, 6 legs 77%, 7 legs 69%, 8 legs 60%, 9 legs 51%, 10 legs 43%.
